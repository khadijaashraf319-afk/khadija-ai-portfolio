The Challenge

I researched common challenges faced by healthcare reception teams and explored how AI could support smoother scheduling and communication workflows.

High volume of routine phone inquiries
Appointment coordination and missed follow-ups
Need for faster, more consistent patient communication
Opportunity to demonstrate voice AI in a practical healthcare context

The Solution

I designed and developed a concept AI Healthcare Receptionist capable of handling incoming calls, collecting appointment details, answering common questions, and integrating with Google Calendar.

The project demonstrates how Voice AI and automation can be combined into a polished, user-friendly experience for healthcare-facing web applications.

Who Can Use This Solution?

Medical Clinics

Automate patient communication, appointment scheduling, and common inquiries.

Handle bookings, appointment reminders, and routine patient questions.

Physiotherapy Centers

Eye Care Clinics

Provide instant responses and simplify appointment management.

Private Healthcare Providers

Deliver a professional AI-powered reception experience that scales with patient demand.

Every technical hurdle became an opportunity to refine the experience.
